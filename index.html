Börsen Überblick

Börsen Überblick creates one short video per trading day with what matters on the stock market today: scheduled economic data, company earnings, overnight moves and stocks with notable trends, based on public market data.

No investment advice – information only about what is currently happening on the market.
Keine Anlageempfehlung – nur Information, was gerade am Markt passiert.

Connect your TikTok account

  1. Click Continue with TikTok and approve access.
  2. Every trading day in the morning, the video is uploaded to your TikTok inbox as a draft.
  3. You get a TikTok notification, review the draft, edit the caption and decide yourself whether to publish it.

Requested permissions: user.info.basic (show which account is connected) and video.upload (upload drafts). Nothing is published without your action.
