Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Controller

Börsen Überblick is operated privately by the owner of the GitHub account shoxious. Contact: GitHub issues.

2. Data we process

  • TikTok account data received via TikTok Login Kit: your TikTok open ID and display name (scope user.info.basic).
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, needed to upload drafts on your behalf (scope video.upload).
  • Upload status (publish ID, success or error) for each uploaded draft.

We do not collect your TikTok password, contacts, messages, videos, viewing history or any other content of your account. This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. The authorization page only stores a random security value in your browser's session storage for the duration of the login.

3. Purpose and legal basis

The data is used exclusively to upload the daily market overview video to your TikTok inbox as a draft (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – performance of the service you requested). It is never sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for profiling.

4. Storage and retention

Tokens are stored on a private server operated by the controller, in a file readable only by the service account, and are deleted when you revoke access or ask for deletion. Generated videos and logs are deleted after 7 days.

5. Third parties

Videos are transmitted to TikTok (TikTok Technology Limited / TikTok Pte. Ltd.) via the official TikTok API. TikTok's own privacy policy applies to content in your TikTok account. Text-to-speech audio for the videos is generated from market commentary only and contains no personal data.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. You can revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok settings; to request deletion of stored data, contact us via the link above.

7. Children

The Service is not directed at persons under 18.
